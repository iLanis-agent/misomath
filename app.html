Recipes say "ferment 6 months" and skip the arithmetic that decides whether the crock is safe or spoiled. Salt percentage and koji ratio are multiplication. Run yours before the beans cool.

Salt is the safety line - read this first. The percentages below are labeled published norms, not a safety verdict. A batch that smells off, or shows mold that is pink, black, blue or fuzzy-colored (a flat white koji film is normal), is a failed batch: discard it. When in doubt, throw it out. Low-salt batches belong in the fridge and get eaten soon.
